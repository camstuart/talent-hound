## Initiatives

An initiative is one piece of work with one goal. There are three kinds: a job search for one candidate, a talent search for one role, and business development.

An initiative can be archived when the work ends. Archiving hides it from the list without deleting anything — an archive is a record that the work happened, which is why it still blocks deleting the people it references.

## Records are shared

Candidates, companies, contacts, and roles belong to the workspace, not to an initiative. A candidate you added while searching for a role is the same candidate next month in a different search, with the same profile and the same history.

This is why deleting an initiative does not delete its candidates: they were never inside it.

## What each record holds

- **Candidate** — names, contacts, location, work rights, availability, employment and pay expectations, and where those facts came from.
- **Company** — name, website, notes.
- **Contact** — a person at a company.
- **Role** — a title, a company, and whether it came from a search or from you.

## Contacts at a company

The contacts-at-company view counts only that company's people. It is a count of what you know, not an estimate.
